Our Academic Foundation

Bridging Regional Talent to Global Careers

Established under the S. Sukhjinder Singh Memorial Educational Society, SGI Gurdaspur is one of the most comprehensive private educational complexes in the Punjab–Himachal border belt. Located in Hayat Nagar, Hardochhani Road, the institution empowers students from Gurdaspur, Batala, Pathankot, Himachal Pradesh, J&K, and international cohorts with premier professional qualifications.

Recognized by National Education Awards for regional academic impact, SGI pairs university curricula across Engineering, Pharmacy, Nursing, Allied Health Sciences, Management, and Polytechnic with clinical hospital internships, smart laboratories, and IT coding workshops.
