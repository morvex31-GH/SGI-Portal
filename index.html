Legacy & Leadership

About SGI Gurdaspur

Pioneering technical, managerial, and healthcare education in the Majha region under the Sukhjinder Memorial Education Society.

Our Foundation

Empowering Youth Through Technical Skill

Sukhjinder Group of Institutes was established with a clear mandate: to provide top-tier, industry-ready higher education to students across Punjab without requiring them to relocate to distant metros. Located on Hardochhanni Road in Hayat Nagar, Gurdaspur, the campus brings together state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure, university-vetted academic rigor, and professional mentoring.

Over the last two decades, SGI has expanded from an engineering and polytechnic focus to encompass pharmaceutical sciences, nursing, paramedical diplomas, business management, and computer applications.
